Privacy Policy for SizeMint

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

SizeMint ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how SizeMint handles information when you use our Android application.

By using SizeMint, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Files and Photos

SizeMint allows you to select and process photos and PDF files on your device.

We do not upload, store, or transmit your photos or PDF files to our servers.

Files selected by you are processed locally on your device for functions such as resizing, compressing, or reducing file size.

We do not have access to the contents of the photos or PDF files you process.

Personal Information

SizeMint does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or address to use the core features of the App.

We do not operate our own server for storing your files or personal information.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies.

3. Permissions

Internet Access

The App uses Internet access primarily to allow Google AdMob to request and display advertisements.

Photos and Files

When you select a photo or PDF, SizeMint uses Android's system file/photo selection mechanisms to allow you to choose the file you want to process.

The App does not require broad access to your entire photo gallery or file storage for its core functionality.

Selected files are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

4. Data Storage and Retention

SizeMint does not maintain a server or cloud storage system for your photos, PDFs, or other files.

We do not retain copies of files that you process through the App.

Processed files remain under your control on your device and are saved only when you choose to save or export them.

SizeMint is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.
