Wave Zero: Zombie Siege – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Wave Zero: Zombie Siege (“the game”, Android package com.rahuldev.zombiehunting) uses, and why. The game is made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”).

Short version

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your player name, scores, progress and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your phone so you can keep playing: the player name you choose, your character, best score, game settings (music, sound, vibration, steering) and simple counters (for example, how many runs you have started). This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads are shown between runs and, if you choose, as a video you can watch to get an extra life. To serve ads, AdMob may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system version, language);
  • how you interact with ads (for example impressions and taps);
  • diagnostic information (for example crash and performance data related to ads).

Google uses this information to show ads, measure how ads perform, and prevent fraud and abuse. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalised. Data is encrypted in transit. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): when required, the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy Options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Deleting data: uninstalling the game removes everything it stored on your device. Data held by Google for advertising is managed through your advertising ID and Google account settings.

Permissions

The game uses internet and network-state access to load ads, and vibration for game feedback (which you can turn off in Settings). It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
